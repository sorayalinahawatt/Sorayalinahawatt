Biography

Bridging Perspectives on the Global Stage

Soraya Lina Hawatt is a student of International Relations with a minor in Law at UM6P. She is a recognized youth climate leader and an active advocate for international dialogue and diplomacy.

Raised in Australia with a strong intercultural background, her upbringing deeply informs her approach to global affairs. As an international debater, she has represented bilateral perspectives in competitive championships, developing a sharp, analytical voice in public discourse.

Looking toward future public leadership, Soraya combines intellectual seriousness with strategic ambition, aiming to contribute significantly to African geopolitics, climate policy, and international cooperation.
